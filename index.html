Edan Toledo

PhD researcher · FAIR at Meta & UCL

My work focuses on autonomous research agents, continual learning, and reinforcement learning. I co-initiated the AIRA series, developing agents that conduct research over long time horizons.

I am part of the FAIR PhD Program, supervised by Pontus Stenetorp and Martin Josifoski. Previously, I was a research engineer at InstaDeep, working on reinforcement learning.

Selected research

  • AIRA3: Gold in a Live Kaggle Competition

    Co-first author·2026

    To our knowledge, AIRA3 is the first autonomous system to win Gold in a live featured Kaggle competition. It finished 8th of roughly 4,000 teams in NVIDIA’s Nemotron reasoning challenge.

    Meta announcement

    Coverage: AlphaSignal

  • AIRA2: Overcoming Bottlenecks in AI Research Agents

    Co-first author·NeurIPS 2026

    AIRA2 resolved bottlenecks in compute, evaluation, and agent capability, establishing scaling laws for research agents that transfer across language models. It achieved state-of-the-art performance on MLE-bench-30, with sustained gains over 72-hour searches.

    paper

    Coverage: Forbes · AI Accelerator Institute

  • AIRA1: Search, Exploration, and Generalization in MLE-bench

    Co-first author·NeurIPS 2025 (Spotlight)

    AIRA1 established search and exploration methods that raised the MLE-bench lite medal rate from 39.6% to 47.7%, setting a new state of the art. The accompanying AIRA-dojo framework is now used for production kernel optimisation and automated architecture discovery at Meta.

    paper·code

  • Omnilingual MT: Machine Translation for 1,600 Languages

    RL training·EMNLP 2026 (Oral)

    I was responsible for all RL training of Omnilingual MT, spanning 1,600 languages.

    paper
